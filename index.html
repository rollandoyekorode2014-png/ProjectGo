Our Story

Crafted with Passion

Founded in 2018 by Chef Marco Bianchi, ProjectGo began as a small bistro with a big dream — to redefine modern fine dining through bold flavors and heartfelt hospitality.

Today, we source the finest seasonal ingredients from local farms and global artisans, creating dishes that celebrate both tradition and innovation. Every plate is a canvas, every meal a memory.
